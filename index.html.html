      What this model assumes
      
      
        
    • Ramp radius 22 m, ending 120° around the curve, so the car finishes past the vertical wall and upside down. Drawn to look like the ramp in the demo photos. The real size is not published.
      • 
        
    • Tyre grip μ = 1.1, a typical value for performance tyres. Motor push limit 1.7 g, worked out from Tesla’s quoted 0 to 60 mph in under 1.6 s (26.8 m/s ÷ 1.6 s).
      • 
        
    • The car is treated as a single point 0.42 m above the surface, 4.2 m long on screen, with no air drag. Everything is measured in multiples of the car’s weight, so the weight cancels out.
      • 
        
    • Fan suction is whatever you set with the slider. Thrusters only control spin, so the model needs no thrust figure. In the demo Tesla says thrusters also launched the car off the ramp.
      • 
        
    • One report says the demo car was remote-operated. Here the car follows a simple cruise-control rule.
